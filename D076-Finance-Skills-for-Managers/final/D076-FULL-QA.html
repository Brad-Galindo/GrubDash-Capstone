A company called Bobby’s Books is considering purchasing a new bookbinding machine. The company calculates the hurdle rate of the project to be 9% and the IRR to be 11%. Should the company purchase the bookbinding machine?

Answer: Yes, because the IRR exceeds the cost of capital.

Why: When the IRR of a project is greater than the hurdle rate (the required rate of return, or cost of capital), it indicates that the company should accept the project.

Q231

A company is considering five projects that are not mutually exclusive. However, the company does not have enough money to do all of them. In order to prioritize projects that fit within the company’s budget, which capital budgeting method should be used?

Answer: Profitability index (PI)

Why: The PI should be used first to compare the projects and then to rank them to maximize the value of the firm.

Q232

A company is trying to decide which of four projects to invest in.

Project 1 has an IRR of 14% and an NPV of $54,000. Project 2 has an IRR of 11% and an NPV of $67,000. Project 3 has an IRR of 9% and an NPV of $60,000. Project 4 has an IRR of 13% and an NPV of $47,000.

If the company can do only one project, which project should it choose to add the greatest value to the firm?

Answer: Project 2

Why: The project with the highest NPV will bring the most value to the company.

Q233

A financial analyst for the company Bobby’s Books has been asked to evaluate a potential investment using a method that considers the time value of money. Is there more than one way to do this?

Answer: Yes, the analyst could use both the NPV and the IRR.

Why: Both NPV and IRR take into account the time value of money.

Q234

A potential project to expand the size of an apartment complex will cost $100,000. Its calculated net present value is $5,000. Given this information, which statement is correct?

Answer: The project should be accepted because it has a positive NPV.

Why: Because the NPV is positive, the project should be accepted.

Q235

Alphabet Co. has $50,000 to spend on capital investment projects for the next year. It will do as many projects as it has cash for. Alphabet Co. calculates the potential incremental cash flows and costs of the projects as well as the NPV, IRR, and PI for each project. How should the company decide which projects to invest in if it wants to maximize the total amount of value created?

Answer: It should choose the projects with the highest PIs until all capital has been used.

Why: By choosing the projects with the highest PI, Alphabet Co. will be able to use its limited capital effectively to create the most overall value for the firm.

Q236

An investor just purchased a bond for $973 that has a par value of $1,000. What type of bond is this?

Answer: A discount bond

Why: When the market price is less than the par price of a bond, you know that the YTM is currently higher than the coupon rate of that particular bond, so it is being sold at a discount.

Q237

Beckingham Sports is an American sporting goods company. Based on a $400,000 market study and a $600,000 fee for consulting spent prior to the project, the firm can increase its annual operating cash flow by $3,000,000 by selling overseas. Because the firm was considering the expansion, it spent $2,000,000 to purchase a land for new factory and equipment. However, someone is making an offer to pay the company $3,000,000 for the land it purchased for the new factory. What is relevant to include in the company’s capital budgeting decision?

Answer: 3,000,000 for the offer price of the land

Why: If you do the project, you will miss the opportunity to sell the land for $3,000,000. This is an example of an opportunity cost.

Q238

How are non-incremental cash flows different from incidental cash flows?

Answer: Incidental cash flows are indirect cash flows that are not explicitly revenues or costs. Nevertheless, they must be included in the analysis.

Why: Incidental cash flows are included in analysis and non-incremental cash flows are not.

Q239

How can having more debt benefit a company?

Answer: Interest expense on debts is paid before taxes are calculated.

Why: This describes the tax shield advantage of debt.

Q240

How do corporations and purchasers of financial securities view returns?

Answer: Purchasers of financial securities look at returns as the amount of money they require in order to lend or give their money to the corporation that issued those securities.

Why: This is a correct description of returns from the viewpoint of investors.

Q241

How do you factor sunk costs into capital investment analysis?

Answer: For the purposes of analysis, sunk costs are irrelevant.

Why: Sunk costs are costs that have already been incurred whether you choose to do a project or not.

Q242

How does allocated overhead affect the selection of capital investment projects?

Answer: These cash flows are not a direct result of a specific project but are a general cost to the firm.

Why: These cash flows are irrelevant to your analysis.

Q243

How does cannibalization factor into capital investment decisions?

Answer: If your company is planning on launching a product, and that product is going to steal some of the sales of another of the company’s products, that loss of sales could be an incidental cost or revenue caused by the new product.

Why: This is the correct definition of cannibalization.

Q244

How does management choose between two projects that are seemingly the same?

Answer: Management can analyze the different inherent risks that change the cost of capital to the firm.

Why: Each project will have its own inherent risks.

Q245

How does the PI aid in interpretation of the NPV?

Answer: It gives an idea of the return generated by a project.

Why: The PI scales different-sized projects so that their returns are comparable.

Q246

If two projects are mutually exclusive, which decision-making criterion will help you make the best decision about which project to accept?

Answer: Net present value (NPV)

Why: When only one project can be chosen, the PI is not useful because it does not indicate the dollar value that a project will add to or take away from a firm.

Q247

In what way is preferred stock different from bonds?

Answer: Companies are allowed to skip payments to preferred stockholders but not to bondholders.

Why: If companies skipped bond payments, they would go into default. Companies can skip payments for preferred stock. However, by skipping these payments, companies are not able to pay common stockholders and must pay dividends in arrears at some point.

Q248

Should a firm accept a project that has a PI of 0.8? Why?

Answer: No, because the project would be generating cash inflows that are 20% short of the initial investment.

Why: As a rule, firms should accept only projects that have a PI greater than 1.

Q249

Suppose Alice is trying to explain to her friend, who knows nothing about the time value of money, why she should invest in Alice’s new company. Which method of valuation should Alice use to convince her friend to invest?

Answer: Internal rate of return (IRR)

Why: The IRR is easy to interpret, which makes it ideal for communicating the potential of an investment decision.

Q250

Why: IRR calculations are represented as percentages because they are rates of return.

Q251

Talia is comparing four mutually exclusive projects. In order to choose the best project to optimize the goal of the firm, which capital budgeting method should Talia use?

Answer: Net present value (NPV)

Why: When you compare mutually exclusive projects, you should look at how much value is added by each project, because you can do only one of them. Therefore, you should use the NPV method to choose a project.

Q253

The YTM of a bond went from 8% to 7%. What can be predicted about the price of the bond?

Answer: It will increase.

Why: There is an inverse relationship between YTM and the price of a bond.

Q254

What are incremental cash flows?

Answer: Any additional cash flows, whether in or out of the firm, that are created as a result of accepting a project

Why: This is the correct definition of incremental cash flows.

Q255

What indicates to a firm that a project will increase shareholder wealth?

Answer: The NPV is positive.

Why: The NPV is an estimate of the dollar amount that would be added to the firm’s value as a result of the investment.

Q256

What is a disadvantage of using the NPV method?

Answer: It is not an effective way to compare projects of different sizes.

Why: NPV should not be used to compare projects of different sizes.

Q257

What is an advantage of using the NPV method?

Answer: It calculates the dollar value that would be added to the firm by doing the project.

Why: The NPV is expressed as a dollar amount, and the result of the NPV calculation is exactly how much value would be added to the firm from that specific project.

Q258

What is an opportunity cost?

Answer: The loss of the ability to use an asset toward the next best project once you have invested it in another project

Why: The concept of opportunity cost is that because you use an asset to invest in one project, you lose the opportunity to use the same asset to do a different project.

Q259

What is opportunity cost as it relates to the time value of money?

Answer: It is the opportunity you forgo to invest in other options due to the time scope of an investment.

Why: Opportunity cost is the cost that you have to give up for something as a result of investing in something else.

Q260

What is the disadvantage of debt financing?

Answer: Debt financing does not actually achieve an optimal capital structure for a company.

Why: Debt creates a tax shield, but there should be a mixture of debt and equity in an optimal capital structure.

Q261

What is the name for the process of evaluating and planning for purchases of long-term assets?

Answer: Capital budgeting

Why: Capital budgeting is the process of evaluating and planning for purchases of long-term assets.

Q262

What is the relationship between the risk and the rate of return?

Answer: The higher the risk investors have to take on, the higher return they require.

Why: Investors will take on more risk if there is potential for a higher return.

Q263

What must be determined in order to compare the values of two projects with differently timed cash flows that does not need to be determined for projects with similarly timed cash flows?

Answer: Opportunity cost

Why: Opportunity cost must be determined to analyze two such projects.

Q264

What part of the NPV calculation is very important but difficult to estimate?

Answer: The cost of capital

Why: The cost of capital is affected by several things, such as different capital structures, timing of cash flows, and investment potential, which makes it difficult to calculate. An inaccurate prediction of the cost of capital may cause a firm to miss out on good projects or accept bad projects.

Q265

What would an analyst predict for a potential investment with an NPV of zero?

Answer: The project would earn exactly the rate of return required by the firm.

Why: An NPV of zero indicates that a project will neither add nor take away value from a firm.

Q266

Which capital investment evaluation method is presented as a ratio?

Answer: Profitability index (PI)

Why: The PI is the ratio of discounted benefits to discounted costs.

Q267

Which condition indicates that an investment will add value to a company?

Why: If the project provides more value than it costs, then mathematically it will add value to the company.

Why: This correctly characterizes a finance decision.

Q275

Why is it appropriate to calculate the value of a bond in the same way that the present value of an annuity is calculated?

Why: Bond cash flows are an annuity, a constant amount paid every period.

Why is it appropriate to calculate the value of a preferred stock in the same way that you would find the present value of a perpetuity?

Why: This is the case for preferred stocks, which are a type of perpetuity.

Q277

Why is it important to consider all relevant cash flows in an ideal evaluation method for capital investment?

Answer: Without considering every cash flow of a potential project, you do not know how the project will enhance the value of a firm.

Why: You need to include all the cash flows coming from a potential project to understand how much value they will add to the firm.

Q278

Why is it important to consider the cost of capital in an ideal evaluation method of capital investment?

Answer: Because cash flows for a project may be uncertain

Why: This is the reason why you should consider the cost of capital.

Q279

Why is it important to consider the time value of money in an ideal evaluation method for capital investment?

Answer: Because the value of a cash flow today is different from the value of a cash flow of the same dollar amount in 10 years

Why: You cannot directly compare dollar amounts received at different times.

Q280

Why is it important to have an accurate, carefully calculated required rate of return as part of the NPV?

Answer: An inaccurate required rate estimate could cause a firm to reject good projects or accept bad projects.

Why: While the required rate of return is the most difficult part of the NPV calculation to estimate, it is also the most important.

Q281

Why is NPV the most reliable method for evaluating investments?

Answer: It considers the time value of money, it tells you the dollar value that the investment will add to the firm, and it takes risk into account.

Why: These are the main advantages of the NPV, some of which are not included in other methods of valuation, which makes NPV the most reliable.

Q282

Why is the IRR a poor valuation method for a project with unconventional cash flows?

Answer: There are multiple sign changes in the calculation resulting in multiple IRRs, and it is impossible to tell which IRR is the correct one.

Why: Projects with unconventional cash flows must be valued using NPV.

Q283

Why is the timing of cash flows an important characteristic of capital investment?

Why: The cash flows of an investment need to be compared to the cash flows of other projects.

Q284

Why is there always a cost for bringing funds into a business?

Why: Investors need a reason to part with their money.

Q285

Q286

Q287

Why might a firm seek capital investment?

Answer: To purchase long-term assets for future growth

Why: Firms need funding for projects that will increase shareholder wealth in the future.

Q288

You are considering a project that has a profitability index of 1. What does this mean?

Answer: The project has the internal rate of return equal to the cost of capital.

Why: PI = 1 means that the break-even point is the estimated cost of capital; in other words, the cost of capital and the rate of return should be exactly the same.

Q290

Why: Common stock valuation uses the constant growth model under the assumption that the growth rate is constant forever.

Q291

You calculate the PI of a project to be 1 but realize that some aspect of your calculation was incorrect and needs to be adjusted. Which adjustment to the PI estimation should cause you to reject the project?

Answer: The cost of capital was underestimated, so you adjust the cost of capital to be higher.

Why: Increasing the cost of capital decreases the PI. Therefore, the new PI will be less than 1, and you should reject the project.
